# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent to each other. Amateur radio has a long tradition of people helping
strangers get on the air; that is the tone here.

## What is expected

- Assume the other person is trying to help.
- Critique code, not people. "This drops frames when the index wraps" is useful.
  "This is amateur work" is not.
- Accept that "no" is a complete answer to a feature request, and that a maintainer
  with one pair of hands will sometimes be slow.
- Newcomers ask questions that seem obvious. Answer them anyway, or point at the
  document that does.

## What is not

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information — including callsigns, station locations
  or grid squares — without their consent. In this project that matters more than
  most: a callsign is a legal identity tied to a physical address.
- Sustained disruption: reopening settled decisions, or arguing past the point of
  usefulness.

## Scope

This applies in the repository — issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages
— and anywhere someone is representing the project.

## Reporting

Raise it with the maintainer privately through
[GitHub](https://github.com/gauthig). Reports will be handled discreetly, and the
reporter's identity will not be shared without their agreement.

Responses run from a private word, through having a comment removed, to being blocked
from the repository, depending on what happened.
